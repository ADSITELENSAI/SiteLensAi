Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

When you use SiteLens, we collect your email address (when you sign up), the website URLs you submit for analysis, and your scan results. We do not collect payment information directly — that is handled securely by Stripe.

How we use your data

We use your email to manage your account and send you reports you request. We use your scan history to show you progress over time in your dashboard. We do not sell your data to third parties, ever.

Website analysis

SiteLens only analyzes publicly visible information on websites you submit — the same information any visitor or search engine can see. We do not access private pages, backends, or any data behind a login.

Data storage

Your account data is stored securely using Supabase, a SOC 2 compliant database provider. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Cookies

We use a session token stored in your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at hello@sitelens.io. We will process all deletion requests within 7 business days.

Contact

For any privacy concerns, contact us at hello@sitelens.io
